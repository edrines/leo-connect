ABOUT LEO CONNECT

A club where connection becomes hope.

Leo Connect exists to bring people together, care for those in need and create a community where everyone can experience dignity, encouragement and belonging.

01

WHY WE EXIST

No one should feel forgotten.

Life can become difficult, isolating and uncertain. Leo Connect responds with something simple but powerful: people who are willing to listen, care and stand together.

VISION

A community where hope is shared freely.

We want to see people connected to caring communities that encourage them through difficult seasons and celebrate their progress.

MISSION

Connect. Care. Serve. Give hope.

We bring people together and turn compassion into meaningful relationships, practical service and encouragement for those in need.
